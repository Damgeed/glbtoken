Refund Policy

Token Purchases
All token purchases are final. Tokens that have been used (consumed via API or chat) cannot be refunded.

Unused Tokens
If your account is active and tokens have not been used, please contact support@glbtoken.com for a case-by-case review.

Service Downtime
In the event of extended service downtime (>48 hours), we may issue token credits at our discretion.

Crypto Payments
Crypto payments are processed on-chain and are generally non-reversible. Please verify all transaction details before sending.

Chargebacks
Chargebacks will result in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of all token balances.
